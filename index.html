Before calling an agreement bankable

  1. Confirm the legal signing entity and whether a group/council actually guarantees its rent. A nomination or care contract is not a landlord rent guarantee.
  2. Get written acceptance of location, unit sizes, use, licensing/EPC standards, client cohort and the full 5-30-unit tranche.
  3. Verify agreed base rent, reviews/caps, payment start, void cover, benefit-risk allocation, repairs, insurance and compliance responsibilities.
  4. Read audited cash/liquidity, unrestricted reserves, debt/lease liabilities, regulator judgements and funding concentration, for the signing entity, not only its group.
  5. Match lease duration and break clauses to commissioning/referral arrangements. Identify who pays during gaps and on early termination.
  6. Require solicitor and lender review, security/guarantees where appropriate, stress tests and a viable alternative letting plan.
