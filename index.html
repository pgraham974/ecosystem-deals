Distress classes: distressed = receiver/administration/winding-up filed; strained = private-individual lender, bridging loan older than 15 months, second charge, or overdue accounts; pressured = specialist lender or new charge. Pressure = Companies House signals: specialist or bridging lender, new charge in the last 18 months, overdue filings, insolvency. Costs are EPC indicative midpoints.
